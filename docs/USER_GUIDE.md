# Parichay User Manual & Guide (USER_GUIDE.md)

Welcome to Parichay. This manual guides you through creating, designing, and sharing your offline digital business card across iPhone, Android, and Web.

---

## 1. Creating and Editing Your Business Card

### Steps:
1. Open the **Card** screen. Tap **"Edit Card"** or the pencil icon.
2. Enter your core identity:
   - **First Name & Last Name** (Required).
   - **Job Title & Company** (Optional).
3. Add your contact endpoints:
   - **Phone**: Select label (Mobile, Work, Personal) and enter your number. Parichay automatically formats the country code.
   - **Email**: Add your primary email address.
   - **Websites & Socials**: Add your portfolio, LinkedIn, GitHub, X (Twitter), or WhatsApp link.
4. **Photo / Avatar**:
   - Tap the camera placeholder to upload a photo.
   - The photo is cropped into a high-density square and stripped of all EXIF metadata on your device.
5. Tap **"Save Card"**. Your offline QR matrix generates immediately.

---

## 2. Designing with Style Studio

Navigate to the **Studio** tab in the bottom bar to customize the visual look of your card:

1. **Presets**:
   - Choose from 17 curated themes (e.g. *Terracotta Ember*, *Obsidian Gold*, *Emerald Monogram*, *Minimal Editorial*).
2. **Typography**:
   - Select from 45+ self-hosted open-source typography fonts spanning serif, sans-serif, monospaced, display, and Devanagari scripts.
3. **Module & Finder Geometry**:
   - Choose dot patterns: *Dots*, *Rounded*, *Square*, *Extra-Rounded*.
   - Choose corner eyes: *Circle*, *Square*, *Dot*.
4. **Center Icon**:
   - Display your photo avatar, initials monogram, or leave clean.
5. **Optical Scan Verification**:
   - Watch the live indicator at the top of the Studio. Parichay tests each color and shape combination using an internal optical scanner to verify that standard mobile cameras can read it easily.

## 3. Sharing Your Card

### A. Fullscreen QR Presentation
- Tap **"Fullscreen QR"** on the home screen.
- The app sets maximum white contrast and keeps the screen awake.
- The recipient simply opens their standard iPhone Camera or Google Lens and points at your screen.
- Tap anywhere or swipe down to dismiss.

### B. High-Resolution Exports
- Navigate to **Card &rarr; Share &amp; Export**:
  - **Save PNG (1200px)**: Perfect for presentation slides and website badges.
  - **Save SVG (Vector)**: Infinite resolution for graphic design.
  - **Printable Card (300 DPI)**: Standard 3.5" x 2.0" business card layout with front and back plates.
  - **Export .vcf File**: Direct contact file for email signatures.

## 4. Setting Lock-Screen Wallpapers

Keep your business card reachable even when your phone is locked:

1. Navigate to **Card &rarr; Lock Screen Wallpaper**.
2. Select your backdrop theme (Card Style, Charcoal, Navy, Warm).
3. **Platform-Specific Application**:
   - **Android**: Tap **"Set Lock Screen (Android Native)"** or **"Lock + Home"**. The wallpaper is applied immediately without leaving the app. If your device has custom OEM theming, tap **"System Picker"**.
   - **iPhone (iOS)**: Tap **"Save Wallpaper Image"**. Open iOS **Settings &rarr; Wallpaper &rarr; Add New Wallpaper &rarr; Photos**, select the image, and apply as a Wallpaper Pair. The QR is carefully aligned below the clock safe zone.

---

## 5. Saving to Google Wallet & Apple Wallet

1. Navigate to **Card &rarr; Save to Wallet**.
2. **Android**:
   - Tap **"Add to Google Wallet"**.
   - Parichay saves a 1080x1350 optimized photo pass to your gallery and launches Google Wallet.
   - In Google Wallet, tap **Add to Wallet &rarr; Photo**, select the Parichay card pass, and save.
3. **iPhone**:
   - Tap **"Save Pass Image"**.
   - View the pass in Apple Photos or add to your Favourites album for lock-screen widget access.

---

## 6. Backups & Data Erase

1. **Export Backup**:
   - Open **Settings &rarr; Export Card Backup (.json)**.
   - Saves a complete, offline JSON file containing your card details, styles, and photo.
2. **Import Backup**:
   - Open **Settings &rarr; Import Card Backup** and select your `.json` file to restore.
3. **Erase All Data**:
   - Open **Settings &rarr; Erase All Data**.
   - Confirms and completely removes all contact data, cached styles, and photos from device memory.
